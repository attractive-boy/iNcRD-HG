# Functional-target-mediated morphological alignment, v1

This is a prospective independent adaptation of **Lai et al., Multimodal contrastive learning for integrating molecular representations and cellular phenotypes in drug-target interaction prediction**, *Bioinformatics* (2026), DOI [10.1093/bioinformatics/btag368](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btag368), PMID 42635222, PMCID PMC13501321. The title, authors and journal were checked against PubMed, exact Crossref DOI metadata and the lawful Europe PMC full text. Sections 2–4, all method equations, the released image model/dataset/loss/trainer/config, the multi-positive loss and the image tutorial were read. The author repository is YJRubyLai/Unified-DTI; its exact commit and verified Git blobs are retained in the source manifests. No author code or pretrained weights were executed.

The new hypothesis is that a drug/CRISPR morphological space supervised by external positive drug–target interactions can transfer through **functional miRNA target sets** and improve MiDrug residual ranking. This is one mechanism family. The new data source, contrastive objective, target-set proxy, prediction residual, null branch and controls are not separate innovations. It is distinct from replacing a tabular foundation model, extending graph36/tiered62/context76 columns, classic anchor shrinkage, and frozen REIFM plus attribute kernels. Earlier expression-to-viability/gene-effect regression does not establish efficacy for the present microscopy modality.

## Source boundaries

- The JUMP dataset repository is fixed at `016e865fa0691244e0860943e41c7d6a88ed2580`. Its official profile index identifies the compound v1.0 Harmony table (737 coordinates) and the CRISPR v1.0a PCA-corrected table (259 coordinates). HTTP range reads verify object length, ETag and Content-Range; assembled downloads verify MD5 against the single-part S3 ETag and record SHA256.
- The author checkpoint expects 599 CRISPR inputs, whereas the current registered table has 259. The input-coordinate contract and published checkpoint are therefore incompatible; no weight reuse is attempted. Anonymous coordinates are kept in the exact published column order and encoded with modality-specific projections.
- Initial identity audit, before profile availability checks: 41/216 project drugs match exact canonical isomeric SMILES; 7,963 dictionary genes map uniquely through HGNC to the CRISPR metadata; 471/605 miRNAs have at least one mapped functional target. Final measured-profile availability is reported by the input builder, not inferred from metadata alone. Salt forms, stereoisomers and tautomer-specific canonical SMILES are not silently merged.
- The only external interaction labels are the author's released **image-stage training table**. Its gene symbols and canonical structures are mapped to measured profiles and duplicate molecular-identity/gene pairs are collapsed. The author describes these as direct binding interactions; this adaptation does not independently reverify every source assay. The author test table and project evaluation outcomes are not used for this input construction.
- Existing `MiDrug_functional.npz` supplies binary functional-evidence target sets. Binding-only and association-only RNA evidence are not added. A mean of gene knockout embeddings is a model proxy, not an observed miRNA perturbation profile, a sign of regulation, or a drug-resistance measurement.
- Published phenotype tables mix technical sources. Average wells within each perturbation/source and then give each source equal weight. Exclude registered negative/empty controls and nonfinite profile rows. Standardize coordinates using all noncontrol external perturbation aggregates, floor SD at 1e-6 and clip to ±10. The source tables do not support a claim of identical cell-line, dose or treatment-time conditions across chemical and genetic modalities. No such equivalence or causal interpretation is made.

Raw-download timeouts, the initially incorrect repository namespace returning 404, and the successful fixed-blob retries are retained. A timed-out MorphoHELM README and unrelated discovery results are not treated as method evidence. No institutional login or paywall bypass was used.

## Local model and loss

The compound and CRISPR encoders are three-layer projection heads with hidden widths 128 and 64, LayerNorm, ReLU and dropout 0.1, ending in a normalized 64-dimensional vector. The RNA proxy is the normalized mean of encoded, available functional targets. A 64-unit decoder reads RNA, drug, their product and absolute difference. Its final layer starts at exactly zero; the residual is `2*tanh(logit)`. Pairs with no measured drug profile or no available functional target have exactly zero residual.

The phenotype encoder **does not receive project association edges, degree features, original attributes, entity-ID embeddings or validation outcomes**. Five training folds retain the frozen project split indices; their support slots are explicitly empty. Rotating through a training fold controls the BCE query subset, while external phenotype inputs remain fixed. Thus there are no phenotype support graphs to reconstruct or to count as graph audits.

Each of 400 AdamW updates uses:

1. BCE on covered queries in the current training fold, at nested OOF reference logit plus the raw learned residual. Uncovered queries have no trainable residual and are excluded from the BCE normalization.
2. A batch of 256 external positive DTI pairs drawn with replacement from a private generator. Symmetric multi-positive InfoNCE uses cosine similarities at temperature 0.1. The positive mask includes **every known external interaction between sampled endpoints**, including off-diagonal and repeated endpoints. Unannotated in-batch combinations are distractors, not experimentally confirmed negatives.
3. Total loss `BCE + 0.1*alignment`; one backward evaluation and one optimizer update. Weight decay is 0.001 and clipping norm is 5. Learning rates are exactly 0.001/0.003, with checkpoints every 20 updates.

This is a joint objective, not the paper's two-stage structure/image pipeline. It omits ChemBERTa, ESM, structure–image alignment and random-forest classification. The paper states temperature 0.07 and Adam; the released image config/trainer use temperature 0.10, AdamW and cosine scheduling. This local version explicitly uses 0.10 and constant-rate AdamW. It uses a smaller network and a different, public CRISPR preprocessing version. No original-paper performance or exact reproduction is claimed.

Prediction follows the existing strong-reference contract: `reference + 0.1*residual`. The training residual and the fixed prediction coefficient are not presented as calibrated posterior inference. All five empty support slots predict the same value. The LncDrug policy is strict identity, with no model parameters or training; identity records are not neural fits.

## Declared arms and validation

- **full**: correct functional target identities and joint external morphological alignment.
- **no_alignment**: same profiles, architecture, initialization, positive-pair samples and computed auxiliary loss, but multiply that loss by zero. This isolates the effect of external alignment supervision.
- **shuffled_rna_targets**: retain the complete external DTI objective and phenotype tables, but apply one frozen global permutation to the gene coordinates of the RNA target matrix (seed 32016). This preserves each RNA's target count and every pairwise target-set overlap. It preserves the degree multiset, not each named gene's degree. This tests whether correct RNA-to-gene correspondence matters.

All arms have the same parameter and update budgets and consume the same dropout/private-RNG streams. No previously trained model is adopted. Before screening: independent loss/gradient and positive-mask checks, missingness/replay checks, target-set permutation checks, source-label isolation, optimizer/RNG budget checks, source-weighting checks, real training-only short fits and audits of the historical plus three fixed training partitions. A short fit never computes development AUC/AP.

Screening is six new MiDrug fits and six LncDrug identity records. Only a pass of performance, both controls and the zero-residual null permits two further initialization seeds (six more MiDrug fits and six identities). Fixed-partition and frozen outer validation require the dedicated joint-loss fitter. The LncDrug per-seed/per-split/per-metric tolerance remains 0.001. Passing historical or short-run checks is not a completed innovation; all failed versions and reports remain preserved. The development splits overlap and previously viewed outer results do not become an untouched project-wide test set.
